INTRODUCTION: What We Do

B2BAB FOUNDATION short for Blessed to be a Blessing is an NGO committed to empowering rural education through outreach and innovative solutions. We provide essential support, including thrift donations, to create opportunities for underserved children in Nigeria. together, we're shaping brighter futures for rural communities.

What is B2BAB FOUNDATION?

The B2BAB FOUNDATION, short for Blessed 2 be a Blessing, is dedicated to transforming the lives of undeserved communities in rural Nigeria. Our primary focus is on empowering rural education through innovation outreach programs and proving essential resources to bridge the gap in education access. At B2bab Foundation, we believe that education is the key to unlocking brighter futures. Through donations of thrift items, educational materials in remote areas who otherwise have limited access to quality education.


Why You Should Choose Us

We run a dynamic Non-Governmental Organization, we cater for undeserved children, young adult and also distribute thrift items through community engagement. Join us in our mission to uplift lives and provide the tools needed for success. Every small action contributes to a greater cause. Together, we can make a lasting impact on the future of rural communities.
